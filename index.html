Gian Caparas is an aspiring Cloud and Data Engineer currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology at Cebu Institute of Technology – University. He is dedicated to building projects that combine strong functionality with clean, modern design, ranging from fully functional websites to intuitive front-end interfaces. Alongside his academic journey, he also takes on freelance work in web and mobile design, focusing on UI/UX and front-end development.

He has a solid foundation in Object-Oriented Programming using Java and uses Python for data visualization and backend development. He is experienced in creating responsive and dynamic interfaces with React, as well as developing mobile applications using Kotlin. Beyond development, Gian also works with graphic design and video editing, allowing him to produce visually engaging content. With additional experience in copywriting and data entry, he demonstrates strong attention to detail, adaptability, and a continuous drive to grow with emerging technologies.
